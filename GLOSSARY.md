# Parallax Relay
**Gateway**: One of two linked openings that joins distant places. Avoid: teleport button.
**Panel**: A marked surface eligible to hold a gateway.
**Relay cube**: A portable power module used to hold a pressure switch.
**Pressure switch**: A receiver that opens a chamber's exit while a relay cube rests on it.
**Chamber**: A self-contained stage with an objective and exit.
**Crossing**: Passing through a gateway opening into its linked destination.
